How saving works

From an idea to a purchase, in three steps.

  1. 1

    Create a goal

    Name what you want and set the amount you need to reach.

  2. 2

    Save toward it

    Add savings transactions. The app stops you from going over the target.

  3. 3

    Buy it

    When the goal is complete, buy it and your savings turn into a single expense.
